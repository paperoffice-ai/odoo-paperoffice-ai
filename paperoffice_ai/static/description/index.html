PaperOffice AI for Vendor Bills

Read supplier invoices and fill draft vendor bills in Odoo

Attach a supplier invoice (PDF or image) to a draft vendor bill and click Read with PaperOffice AI. PaperOffice AI extracts supplier, invoice number, dates, amounts, line items and the IBAN, and fills the empty fields of the bill. Fields you already filled are never overwritten. The currency is only set while the bill has no lines and still uses the company currency.

Document processing (OCR, extraction, classification) runs on PaperOffice's own hardware in its own data center in Navarra, Spain (EU), without external AI providers.

What the module does

  • Matches the vendor by VAT number or exact company name.
  • Fills vendor reference, invoice date and due date if they are empty, and the currency if the bill has no lines yet.
  • Creates bill lines from the invoice line items, or one line with the net amount, with the purchase tax that matches the VAT rate on the document.
  • Posts the extracted values to the chatter of the bill.
  • Sending takes a few seconds. The result is fetched in the background by a scheduled action and the bill is filled when it is ready.
  • Also available in the vendor bill list for up to 20 draft bills at once.
  • A bill is sent only once. Sending it again needs the separate "Read again" button and a confirmation.
  • Only users with billing rights can send documents.

Checks before you pay

  • Net plus VAT on the document matches the document total.
  • Bill total matches the document total after taxes are applied.
  • Possible duplicate: Odoo's own duplicate warning works as soon as vendor and reference are filled.
  • IBAN on the document is one of the vendor's known bank accounts.

The checks only show warnings on the bill. Nothing is confirmed or paid automatically.

Setup

  1. Create a PaperOffice AI account at paperoffice.ai.
  2. Create a User token (po_ut_...) under Account > API.
  3. In Odoo, open Accounting > Configuration > Settings > PaperOffice AI, switch the feature on and paste the token.

No specific plan is required. Usage is billed to your PaperOffice AI account as usual. The feature is off until an administrator switches it on, and documents are only sent when a user clicks the button.

Limitations and manual attention

  • New vendors are not created automatically. If the vendor is not found, the bill shows a warning.
  • Taxes on created lines are set from the VAT rate on the document when a matching purchase tax exists. If a rate other than 0 % is recognised but no matching purchase tax exists, or the document has several rates, the bill shows a warning. If no rate (or 0 % without a matching tax) is recognised, lines are created without taxes. Review the taxes before confirming.
  • Only draft vendor bills and refunds are processed.

Data protection

Only the attached document of the selected bill is sent to PaperOffice AI. See the PaperOffice AI privacy policy.
